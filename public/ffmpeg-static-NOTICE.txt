Server-side FFmpeg thumbnail encoder

The server-side VP9 encoder uses ffmpeg-static 5.3.0, distributed under the
GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (GPL-3.0-or-later). It runs
only on the application server; the encoder binary is not downloaded by
browsers.
